How to measure

  • Bust: measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.
  • Hips: measure around the fullest part of your hips, feet together.

Fit advice

Between two sizes? For blazers and suits, choose the larger size for comfort over layers. For stretch dresses and skirts, choose the smaller size for a sleek fit. Each product page also describes the fit of that style.

Still unsure? Contact us – we are happy to help you find your perfect fit.

Råd om passform

Mellom to størrelser? For blazere og dresser anbefaler vi den største størrelsen for komfort over andre lag. For kjoler og skjørt i stretch anbefaler vi den minste for en stram passform. Hver produktside beskriver også passformen til plagget.

Fortsatt usikker? Kontakt oss – vi hjelper deg gjerne med å finne perfekt passform.
