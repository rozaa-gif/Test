Orders placed before 13:00 on working days are dispatched the same day. You will receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order is on its way.

Prices for Sweden and Denmark include all duties and taxes – no surprises at the door.

Returns

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you receive your order.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and returned with all tags attached.
  • Register your return via the link in your order confirmation, or email hello@rozathelabel.com.
  • Pack the items securely and attach the return label.
  • We refund to your original payment method within 14 days after receiving your return.

Faulty or damaged items

If something is not right with your order, contact us within a reasonable time after you discovered the problem. Under consumer law you have the right to make a complaint for up to 2 years (5 years for items intended to last significantly longer).
