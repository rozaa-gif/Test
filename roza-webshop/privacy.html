3. How we protect your data

  • All connections to rozathelabel.com are encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes – nobody at ROZA can read them.
  • Card payments are handled by PCI-DSS certified payment providers; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it, protected by two-factor authentication.
  • We collect only the data we need (data minimisation) and delete it when it is no longer needed.

4. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted processors who help us run the shop, under data processing agreements:

  • Shopify – e-commerce platform and hosting
  • Payment providers – [Shopify Payments / Klarna / Vipps MobilePay]
  • Shipping carriers – [Posten/Bring, PostNord]
  • Email service – [Shopify Email / Klaviyo], only if you have subscribed
  • Meta (Instagram) and TikTok – only if you accept marketing cookies

Some providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. In that case we ensure an adequate level of protection, for example through an EU adequacy decision or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Order and accounting records: as required by accounting law (currently 5 years in Norway).
  • Customer account: until you delete it, or after 3 years of inactivity.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Customer service enquiries: up to 12 months after the case is closed.

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time – for cookies via , for emails via the unsubscribe link.

Send your request to privacy@rozathelabel.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the supervisory authority: Datatilsynet in Norway (datatilsynet.no), IMY in Sweden (imy.se) or Datatilsynet in Denmark (datatilsynet.dk).
