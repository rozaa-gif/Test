Q3 Regional Sales Report

Revenue across all regions grew 18 percent this quarter compared to Q2. The North region continued to be the strongest performer, driven largely by the launch of the new premium product line. Customer acquisition costs decreased by 12 percent as the marketing team shifted spend toward higher-performing digital channels.

The South region underperformed expectations, with revenue growth of only 4 percent, well below the company average. Leadership attributes this to increased competition from two new local entrants and a slower rollout of the loyalty program in that territory.

Customer satisfaction scores improved company-wide, rising from 82 to 88 out of 100. Support ticket volume dropped by 9 percent, which the support team credits to the new self-service knowledge base launched in July.

Looking ahead to Q4, the company plans to expand the premium product line into the South and West regions, invest further in the loyalty program, and continue optimizing digital marketing spend. Management expects continued double-digit revenue growth if current trends hold, though rising shipping costs remain a risk to overall margin.

Employee headcount grew by 6 percent this quarter, primarily in customer support and regional sales roles. Retention remained stable at 94 percent, consistent with prior quarters.
